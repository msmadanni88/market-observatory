Read this once. Measured over 850 gaps on 24 markets: 4h filled 55.9 percent of the time, 1d filled 45.8 percent, 50.8 percent overall. That is a coin flip. A kumo gap is a level — a price band the market crossed without trading in — not a signal and not a forecast. It is worth knowing where these bands are, and worth nothing on its own.
